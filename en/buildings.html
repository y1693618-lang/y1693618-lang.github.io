The buildings

All seven buildings and both tap upgrades in one place: price, income per second, time to pay for themselves, and the numbers at ten copies.

Idle income in Crystal Cavern comes from seven buildings. This page lists what each one is, and how much it costs and earns, in numbers. How to decide what to buy is covered in Strategy.

All numbers are base values before cores. If you hold cores, income is higher by that much.

"Pays for itself in" is the price of the first copy divided by its income per second. The stronger buildings further down take longer to earn back their price.

Ten at a time

Each copy of a building costs 15% more than the last. In return, every ten copies double that building's output: ×2 at ten, ×4 at twenty, ×8 at thirty.

For every building, "total for ten" divided by "per second at ten" comes out at almost exactly the same time as the first copy’s payback. The price rise and the doubling are balanced to match. That is why the tenth copy of a building you hold nine of is such a good buy: one purchase doubles that building's entire output.

The two tap upgrades

Besides buildings, two upgrades raise what each tap earns.

UpgradeEffectFirst pricePrice rise
Sharpen the Pick+1 crystal per tap30about 32% each
Resonance GlovesEach tap also adds 3% of your income per second9,00050% each

Sharpen the Pick is available from the start and goes 30, 40, 53, 69 and so on. The Resonance Gloves appear once your all-time total passes 6,000. Gloves stack, 6% for two, 9% for three, so the more your buildings grow, the more each tap is worth.

When you rebirth

Rebirth resets all buildings and both tap upgrades to zero. In return you get cores, which multiply all income. See How rebirth works.
