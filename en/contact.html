Get in touch

Problem reports, requests and anything else.

The address on this page is a placeholder for now and will be replaced with a real one. Until then, mail sent to it will not arrive. Apologies for the inconvenience.

Before you write

Common questions are answered in how to play and strategy. It is worth a look first.

If you are reporting a problem, press "Copy the details" in the game's Menu tab and paste that in along with your message. It makes the problem far easier to reproduce. It contains no personal information.

Form

Write below and press the button, and your mail application opens with the address, subject and body already filled in. Check it over before sending. Nothing is sent from this page directly.

How long a reply takes

Usually within a few days. It is not a good channel for anything urgent.
