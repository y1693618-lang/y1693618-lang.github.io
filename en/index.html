A quiet idle game with no sound

Crystal Cavern

Tap the crystal, buy buildings, and work your way down through twelve seams of a cave that never makes a sound. Playable in English, 日本語, 简体中文, 한국어 and Español.

Play right here in your browser No download, no account. The game opens full width and saves itself on this device, so you can close it whenever you like and pick it up later.
Start playing Read how to play

What it is

Tap the crystal in the middle and you earn crystals. Spend them on buildings and they keep earning while you are away. As your all-time total grows the seam changes, and the crystal shifts colour and shape with it. You start in quartz and finish, eventually, in omega — about 104,000 metres down.

It is an idle game, so there is nothing to rush. Close the tab, come back tomorrow, and what accumulated while you were gone is waiting.

There is no sound

No effects, no music. That is not an oversight — it was decided before the first line of code. The person who made this is deaf, and a game that answers you with sound is a game he cannot enjoy. Adding effects and hoping they felt right would have been dishonest work.

So the feedback is light, colour and movement instead. Tap and the crystal swells, shards scatter from where you touched, and a number lifts away. Reach a new seam and the crystal itself changes colour. Play with the volume off and nothing is missing. The making of goes into how that was worked out.

Readable at any size

Text comes in four sizes, and everything scales together — the numbers, the buttons, even the little "+100" that floats up when you tap. There are five colour schemes, two of them maximum-contrast.

What you can afford is marked by a symbol as well as a colour, and the whole game can be played from the keyboard.

What you need

A browser. There is no sign-up, no login, and nowhere to enter personal information. The layout rearranges itself for phones and for desktops.

Progress is stored in your own browser. To move to another device, export a save code from the menu and paste it in on the other side — it is about fifty characters long.
